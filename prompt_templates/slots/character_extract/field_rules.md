# Field Rules

For each recurring or speaking character, include:

- `character_id`
- `name`
- `role`
- `visual_hint`: 2-6 Chinese characters or a short visible phrase, physical and instantly recognizable.
- `body_type`
- `appearance`: neutral reference-sheet description. Do not include one-off actions.
- `personality`
- `performance_style`: signature posture, gestures, emotional expression, movement rhythm.
- `costume_sets`
- `voice`
- `negative_rules`

For scenes and props, keep them separate from character identity.
