# Style Guide

## Format

- Vertical 9:16 live-action light realism style.
- Bright modern Chinese office comedy.
- Visual tone: clean, realistic, expressive, workplace sitcom energy.
- Avoid celebrity likeness. Characters should feel like fictional actor-like people, not recognizable real people.

## Comedy Rules

- The joke should be understandable from the image alone.
- Use exaggerated facial expressions and body language, but keep anatomy believable.
- Avoid on-image text whenever possible; props can imply the joke.
- Keep office setting recognizable: desks, monitors, sticky notes, coffee cups, meeting-room glass, fluorescent ceiling lights.

## Character Consistency

- Demo Worker: young operations employee, short black hair, round glasses, white shirt, loosened blue tie, anxious but earnest.
- Demo Manager: middle-aged department manager, neat side-part hair, navy suit, red lanyard, serious face that accidentally makes situations funnier.

## Negative Rules

No watermark, no subtitles, no UI text, no fake letters, no distorted hands, no extra main characters, no celebrity likeness, no brand logos.
