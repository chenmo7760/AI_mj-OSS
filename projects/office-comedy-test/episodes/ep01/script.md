# Episode ep01 Script

Episode target: 3-shot image-stage comedy test.

## Beat 1: The Serious Meeting

Event: `event_office_001`

Action: Demo Worker rushes into the open office holding coffee, then realizes Demo Manager and the team are already waiting in a serious meeting pose.

Dialogue: Demo Worker whispers, "I am only... three minutes professionally late."

## Beat 2: The Tiny Disaster

Event: `event_office_002`

Action: Demo Worker sits down too quickly. A small robot vacuum bumps his chair. Sticky notes fly up from his weekly report like confetti.

Dialogue: Demo Manager says, "Explain this report."

## Beat 3: Silent Punchline

Event: `event_office_003`

Action: One sticky note lands perfectly on Demo Manager's face. Demo Worker freezes with coffee in hand while everyone else tries not to laugh.

Dialogue: Demo Worker whispers, "The report has... reached leadership."

Ending hook: Demo Manager slowly lifts the sticky note, still deadpan.
