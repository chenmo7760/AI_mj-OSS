# Episode ep01 Script

## Logline

On a rainy night, Demo Heroine wakes in a cold palace chamber, hides from approaching footsteps, and discovers a clue proving she was brought there on purpose.

## Beats

1. Demo Heroine wakes on an old wooden bed. Rain taps the broken window. Her silver hairpin is the only familiar object.
2. Footsteps approach outside the door. She hears a wooden latch shake.
3. She slips behind a torn bed curtain, gripping the hairpin like a weapon.
4. A tall shadow pauses outside. A palace token glints on the wet floor, suggesting a deliberate trap.

## Dialogue

- Demo Heroine: "Where am I?"
- Demo Heroine: "Someone is outside."
- Demo Heroine: "Stay quiet... breathe."
- Demo Heroine: "This was not an accident."
