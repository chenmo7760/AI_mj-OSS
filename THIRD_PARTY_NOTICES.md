# Dependencies and sources

The core offline validators use Python's standard library. Pillow is an optional dependency
of image helpers, and FFmpeg is a separately installed composition tool; neither is bundled.
Codex and external generation providers are separate products governed by their own terms.

This edition does not vendor external AI video frameworks, their source histories, third-party
media, model weights or private research caches. Referenced product names do not imply
affiliation or upstream maintainer status. Preserve notices when adding third-party content.
